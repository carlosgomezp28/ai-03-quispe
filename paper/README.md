# The paper

The paper for Repository 3 is:

**Quispe, A., & Xu, K. (2026).**  
*Agentic Delegation and the Language Frontier of Software Developers: A Model and Evidence from Claude Code on GitHub.*

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25438
- First submitted: May 25, 2026
- Revised version: July 7, 2026

The repository focuses primarily on the theoretical model:

- language-specific entry thresholds
- feasibility of unfamiliar languages
- conversational AI versus agentic AI
- the activation band
- language-frontier expansion
- comparison with Aouad, Lykouris & Zhong
- skeptical-referee checks of the model and empirical interpretation
